1. Patient & Registration Insights
Insight 1 — Patient acquisition is declining

There are 100 registered patients, but only 1 patient registered in August 2026 up to August 3. Registration volume was much stronger in August 2025 with 13 registrations.

Business insight: Patient acquisition appears to be slowing significantly. The hospital should investigate referral activity, digital marketing, patient reviews, and local outreach.

Insight 2 — Patients are concentrated in a few residential areas

The highest patient concentrations are:

Aliganj — 12 patients
Indira Nagar — 12 patients
Gomti Nagar — 11 patients
Chinhat — 11 patients
Hazratganj — 10 patients

Business insight: These areas can be targeted for local healthcare campaigns, referral programs and location-based digital marketing.

Insight 3 — Senior patients form the largest age segment

Based on the dataset:

Seniors — 42%
Matured (36–55) — 34%
Adults (18–35) — 24%
Under 18 — 0%

Business insight: The hospital has a strong senior-patient base. Preventive health packages, chronic-disease monitoring and regular follow-up programs could be valuable.

3. Treatment Insights
   
Insight 8 — Health Checkups are the most frequent treatment

Top treatments by volume:

Health Checkup — 22
Minor Surgery — 21
X-Ray — 18
CT Scan — 17
Blood Test — 17

Business insight: Health checkups have the highest frequency and could be used as an entry point for preventive-care packages and cross-selling diagnostic services.

Insight 9 — Major and minor surgeries generate high treatment value

Minor Surgery has an average cost of approximately ₹22,376, while Major Surgery averages ₹90,000.

Business insight: Although surgery volume is relatively low, it represents a high-value service category and should be monitored separately from routine treatments.

Insight 10 — Some treatment costs require review

Using the AVG + 1.5 × STDDEV rule, several high-cost treatments are flagged, particularly Minor Surgery and Major Surgery.

Business insight: These records should be reviewed to determine whether the higher cost is legitimate, related to treatment complexity, or caused by billing/data-entry issues.
