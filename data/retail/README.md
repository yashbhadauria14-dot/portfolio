Key Business Insights:

1. Revenue reached ₹83.48 lakh in 2024, with approximately 15.9 lakh units sold.
2. Revenue declined slightly by 1.05% in 2024 compared with 2023, indicating a relatively stable but slightly weakening sales trend.
3. Yogurt is the strongest category, contributing around 40.5% of total revenue and 6.43 lakh units sold.
4. Milk and SnackBar are the next major contributors, each generating roughly 20% of total revenue.
5. Juice is the weakest category, contributing only 2.9% of total revenue, suggesting an opportunity for product improvement, pricing changes, or stronger promotions.
6. SnBrand2 is the top-performing brand, generating approximately ₹14.06 lakh, or 16.8% of total revenue.
7. Promotional sales contribute around 25.3% of total revenue, while non-promotional sales contribute 74.7%, showing that the business is not heavily dependent on  discounts.
8. May and July are the strongest sales months, generating approximately ₹8.02 lakh and ₹8.01 lakh, respectively.
9. November is the weakest month, with revenue of approximately ₹5.81 lakh, indicating a potential seasonal slowdown.
10. Sales are evenly distributed across channels:
E-commerce: 33.46%
Retail: 33.34%
Discount: 33.20%
11. Regional performance is highly balanced, with North, South, and Central each contributing roughly one-third of total revenue.
12. Business opportunity: The company should focus on strengthening the Juice category, improving sales during weaker months, and using targeted promotions to increase revenue without unnecessarily reducing margins.

Strong summary:
“The key insight I found was that although overall revenue remained relatively stable, the business is highly dependent on the Yogurt category, which contributes over 40% of revenue. At the same time, Juice is significantly underperforming. I also identified seasonal fluctuations, with May and July being peak months and November being the weakest. Since regional and channel sales are well balanced, the biggest opportunity appears to be improving category performance and using targeted promotions during weaker periods.”
